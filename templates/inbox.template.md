## System rules (reminder)

- **Force scarcity**: only 1 analytical priority visible per day.
- **Analytical peak**: [user-defined window] → highest-demand task of the day goes here.
- **BRAC block**: 90 min work + 20 min recovery. Max 2–3 weekday, 3–4 protected weekend.
- **Every active task must have a written next physical action** before closing the session.
- **Projects never sit in inbox without decomposition**: a project must always have at least its first atomic next physical action defined.
