## Block-close rule

At the end of each BRAC, **before recovery**, write three lines:

1. **Completed**: what got finished, in one sentence.
2. **Left open**: what is still pending in this task.
3. **Next physical action**: the concrete sentence that will make retaking trivial later.

This is the Zeigarnik discharge ritual. Skip it and the open loop drains working memory through the recovery window.
